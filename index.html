Desk Strike

Shipped & playable

A turn-based 3D physics duel. Two pens face each other on a desk; you flick yours at theirs, and the first pen knocked off loses the round. One gesture controls aim, power and contact point at once. Five pens with genuinely different mass, grip and curve response make pen choice the strategic layer.
